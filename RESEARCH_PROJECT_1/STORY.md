# MARROW: the story (locked 2026-11-15)

## The thing in the lake
Something huge sleeps under Black Hollow Lake. The rattle is its breathing. It trades:
it keeps one person and gives one back.

## The motel
The Marrow Falls Motel was built in the 1970s by researchers who came to study it. Their
records are in the office. The red lanterns were their way of keeping it asleep; the town
still lights them every year and won't say why. The road is closed.

## Cas
Cassidy "Cas" Byrne. Her mother, **Eileen Byrne**, vanished here in **October 1997** (room 104).
Cas researched it for years without telling her best friend (you, "Bug"), then came here in
**2001** to look for her, stayed in room 104, and went missing.

## Timeline
- **Oct 17, 1997:** Eileen Byrne checks into room 104 and is never seen again ("104 - kept").
- **2001:** Cas comes to the motel to find her mother and disappears.
- **2002 (the game):** a year later, you (Bug) come looking for Cas.

## The clue trail
1. **Cas's map**, on the ground by your car, in her handwriting (built).
2. **Room 104**: her wall is all about her *mother*. She never told you.
3. **The office**: the guest register, left open at 1997: *Eileen Byrne - Rm 104*, the next days
   blank, and "104 - kept" in pencil under her name. Then the researchers' records.
4. **The shack**: Cas's notes get stranger, and halfway through **the handwriting changes**.
   It's not hers anymore (second font).
5. **The boathouse**: her boots by the water; the last notes signed "Cas" in the wrong handwriting.

## The world breaks down
The closer you get to the lake, the more the PS2 look falls apart: textures warp, geometry
stretches, the fog turns red (driven by the Fear value in MPC_Horror).

## Ending
The last cabin, the one glowing red: her bed still warm, a lantern lit, a page on the pillow
in the wrong handwriting: *"you came, Bug. now it'll let her go."* The map was bait. Cas, or
whatever is wearing her, traded you for her mother. The rattle is right behind you. Cut to
black.

**Final shot:** a year later, a car pulls into the motel lot at night. On the payphone, a
MISSING poster: yours.

## Decided
- Townspeople are never seen, only felt (lanterns, closed road, things just left).
- No recorded voice: everything is read on paper.
- Messages stay first person (Bug talking).
